DAY-BY-DAY PLAN

4-Day Manali & Solang Itinerary

Designed for scenic mountain viewpoints, comfortable pacing, and great photo spots.

Day 01

Arrival in Manali & Old Manali Walk

Settle into your mountain stay, unwind by the crisp riverside breeze, and explore cozy Old Manali cafés.

Day 02

Sunrise Ridge Viewpoint & Deodar Trail

Catch early mountain light, sip hot masala chai overlooking the valley, and walk through quiet pine paths.

Day 03

Solang Valley & High Mountain Drive

Head toward dramatic ridge views and open mountain bends with plenty of stops for photos and rest.

Day 04

Bonfire Evening & Return Journey

Wrap up with a relaxed morning view, pick up local woolens or snacks, and start your trip back.
